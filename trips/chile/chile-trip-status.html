Expected Weather

Historical averages for late Dec / early Jan. Patagonia weather changes fast — pack for all conditions regardless.

Date Location High Low Conditions
Dec 29 Santiago 28°C / 82°F 14°C / 57°F Hot, dry, sunny. UV is intense.
Dec 30 Casablanca → Valparaiso 22°C / 72°F 13°C / 55°F Cooler coast breeze. Morning fog possible in Casablanca.
Dec 31 Valparaiso 22°C / 72°F 13°C / 55°F Pleasant. Bring a layer for midnight fireworks on the terrace.
Jan 1 Travel → Patagonia — — Santiago airport is warm. Patagonia arrival will feel 15–20° cooler.
Jan 2 Torres del Paine 18°C / 64°F 7°C / 45°F Wind 25–40 mph typical. 34% rain chance. 17h daylight.
Jan 3 Torres del Paine 18°C / 64°F 7°C / 45°F Same. Glacier boat can be cold — wind chill near freezing on the water.
Jan 4 Torres del Paine 18°C / 64°F 7°C / 45°F Same. Gusts can hit 60+ mph — hold doors, secure hats.
Jan 5 Santiago 28°C / 82°F 17°C / 63°F Hot and dry. January is Santiago's warmest month.
Jan 6 Santiago 28°C / 82°F 17°C / 63°F Same. Late checkout + air conditioning recommended.

Key takeaway: Santiago is summer heat (80s°F). Patagonia is brisk hiking weather (40s–60s°F) with serious wind. The temperature swing on Jan 1 travel day is dramatic — dress in layers you can shed or add.
