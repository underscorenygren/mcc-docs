NYE Trip Decision Pre-Plan

Comparing warm-weather options for Dec 29, 2026 – Jan 5, 2027. Last-minute booking, intimate couples trip.

Super El Nino is the wildcard this winter. NOAA has declared a Very Strong El Nino — 69% chance it exceeds anything since 1950. It makes California and the US Southwest wetter, but makes Colombia and the Caribbean drier. Weather risk varies dramatically by destination.

Destination Options

Colombia (Cartagena + Medellin)
IAH → CTG via BOG, ~6h. Open-jaw return MDE → IAH.

El Nino: Drier 7 days. 2 cities. $5.5–9k for two.

The most developed option. Intimate NYE in Cartagena (rooftop dinner + plaza walk at midnight). World-class food scene aligned with both personas. Caribbean seafood is naturally friendly to Lisa's restrictions. Walking food tours, museums, art, coffee farm tour for Erik. No altitude issues (Bogota dropped). Warm weather with Medellin as a perfect-climate decompression leg.

Pros: Best food scene, best El Nino weather, lowest cost, most cultural depth.
Cons: International logistics, Spanish helpful, safety perception, peak-season Cartagena is crowded.
Full research: Colombia Research

Palm Springs
IAH → PSP, ~3.5h direct (American, United).

El Nino: Wetter 3–5 days. Domestic. $2–4k for two.

Short, easy, low-effort. Desert aesthetic, mid-century architecture, good restaurants. But Super El Nino makes this the highest weather risk of any option. Desert soil doesn't absorb rain — flash flooding in a storm. Dec 21–31 is the wettest window. If a storm hits, outdoor activities are wiped out and there's not much to fall back on indoors.

Pros: Shortest flight, simplest logistics, cheapest, familiar (domestic).
Cons: Highest El Nino rain risk, limited indoor fallbacks, less cultural depth.
Full research: Palm Springs Research

Mexico — Tulum / Riviera Maya
IAH → CUN, ~2.5h direct (United, American, Spirit).

El Nino: Likely OK 5–7 days. $4–8k for two.

Short flight, warm, beach + ruins + cenotes. Tulum has a food scene that's exploded in recent years (Arca, Hartwood, Mur Mur). Cenote swimming is unique and low-effort. Chichen Itza or Coba for ruins. NYE is typically beach bonfires and restaurant dinners — can be as intimate as you want. El Nino impact is weaker on the Yucatan than Pacific Mexico.

Pros: Shortest international flight, warm, beach + culture combo, good food scene, cenotes.
Cons: Tulum is trendy/crowded at NYE, prices inflated, some spots feel performative rather than authentic. Lisa's Asian food bar won't be met here.

Mexico — Oaxaca City
IAH → OAX, ~3h (connecting via MEX).

El Nino: Neutral 5–7 days. $3–6k for two.

One of Mexico's best food cities — mole, mezcal, tlayudas, street food culture. Walking food tours are the main activity. Art galleries, textile markets, Monte Alban ruins. Intimate and culturally rich. Not a beach destination — it's a mountain city at ~5,000 ft with pleasant dry-season weather (70s°F). NYE is local and low-key.

Pros: Best food scene in Mexico, walking food tours, mezcal, art/culture, affordable, intimate.
Cons: No beach, connecting flight required, Lisa runs cold (evenings are cool at altitude).

Puerto Rico (San Juan)
IAH → SJU, ~3.5h direct (United, JetBlue).

El Nino: Mixed 5–7 days. $4–7k for two.

No passport needed (US territory). Old San Juan is walkable, colorful, and has a growing food scene. Caribbean food + Spanish colonial architecture + rum distilleries + El Yunque rainforest. NYE in Old San Juan is festive but can be intimate (rooftop restaurant + walk the cobblestones at midnight). Beach day trips to Vieques or Culebra.

Pros: No passport, direct flight, US territory (easy logistics), walkable old city, food scene improving.
Cons: Northern Caribbean may see above-normal rain under El Nino, food scene not as deep as Colombia or Oaxaca.

Curacao or Aruba
IAH → CUR or AUA, ~4.5h (connecting via MIA or direct on some carriers).

El Nino: Drier 5–7 days. $5–8k for two.

Southern Caribbean — outside the hurricane belt and drier under El Nino. Curacao has Willemstad (colorful Dutch colonial, UNESCO), good food scene, and a more authentic feel than Aruba. Aruba is more resort-oriented. Both are warm, dry, and compact. NYE is typically beach/restaurant celebrations.

Pros: Best El Nino weather in the Caribbean, warm, Curacao has cultural depth, outside hurricane belt.
Cons: Connecting flights from Houston, smaller food scene than Colombia, Aruba is resort-heavy.
