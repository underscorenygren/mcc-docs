## Ideas

### What silence sounds like to a machine
Whisper hallucinates "thank you for watching" and "please subscribe" during silence because it was trained on YouTube audio where silent segments are paired with outro subtitles. The model learned that silence = end credits. The Korean/Japanese variants come from fansubbed content. Stats: "thank you" appears in 24.76% of silence hallucinations.

This is a window into how training data shapes model behavior — the machine doesn't know silence is nothing, it thinks silence is the gap between content and credits. What else do models "know" that's actually just statistical residue of their training corpus?
