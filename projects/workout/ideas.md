# Workout App

Gym motivation + guidance app for beginners. Target users: brother and boss (weight loss focus).

## Core concepts

### Levander method
Star-based attendance tracking — reward showing up. Stars accumulate with gym visits, creating visible streaks and momentum. The system rewards consistency over intensity.

### Beginner guidance
"What to do when you get to the gym" — the hardest part for beginners isn't motivation, it's standing in the gym not knowing what to do next. Provide simple, specific routines.

### Food photography → calorie counting
Take a photo of your meal, AI estimates calories. Brother was specifically interested in this. Gemini already does room-correction style analysis — could use similar vision model for food.

## Features brainstorm

- **Attendance tracking** with stars / streaks (Levander method)
- **Beginner workout plans** — simple, specific, "do this today"
- **Photo-based calorie tracking** — snap food, get estimate
- **Weight loss focus** — track progress, set goals
- **Encouragement system** — nudges to go, celebration when you do

## Target users
- Brother — weight loss, food photography/calorie counting
- Boss — gym motivation, beginner guidance
