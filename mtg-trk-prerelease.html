Star Trek (TRK) — Sealed Prerelease Guide

What to build, what to play around, what to first-pick · November 2026

TL;DR — Sealed Priorities

  • Open your bombs first, build around them. Strange New Worlds lands fix mana generously — splashing is encouraged. Don't force two colors if your rares pull you wider.
  • Federation rewards creature-type diversity. In sealed, you naturally get varied creature types across your pool. Lean into it.
  • Gruul landfall and Dimir dilemma control are the strongest shells if your pool supports them.
  • Borg (Orzhov) snowballs. If you see Assimilate pieces, go all-in. If your opponent has them, kill their first Borg creature on sight.
  • White has the best common trick — Set Phasers to... is modal removal at 1 mana. Always respect open W.
  • Red is the deepest trick color — 9 instant-speed cards. Always respect open R mana.
  • Format is midrange. Sealed especially rewards bombs + removal + good mana over speed.
Sealed-specific: You won't draft archetypes — you'll discover them in your pool. Sort by color, identify your bombs and removal, then find which 2-3 color combination gives you the best curve with the most powerful cards. Strange New Worlds lands make 3-color builds very viable.

Key Mechanics

Beam Me Up
Exile a creature, return it later. Reuse ETBs, dodge removal.
Face a Dilemma
Opponent chooses between two bad options. Dimir's core mechanic.
Federation
Bonus when you control creatures with diverse types. Build wide, varied boards.
Strange New Worlds
Explore-style lands. Enter tapped but fix mana and enable landfall.
Assimilate
Borg mechanic. Turns opponents' creatures into Borg. Snowballs hard.
Spacecraft
Vehicle-like artifacts. Crew them for evasive damage.

Bombs — Best Rares & Mythics by Color

Sealed is about bombs. If you open any of these, they should be in your deck — splash if needed. The cards below are the ones you'd be thrilled to crack in your six packs, organized by color to help you evaluate your pool quickly.

Sealed bomb evaluation: In sealed, a bomb needs to win the game on its own or generate overwhelming advantage without requiring other specific cards. Raw power matters more than synergy. Cards that need setup (like Borg payoffs without Borg creatures) are weaker here than in draft.

White Bombs

White's bombs lean towards army-building and protection. Picard is the standout mythic, and white has excellent rare Spacecraft.

Captain Jean-Luc Picard
2WMythic
Other creatures get +1/+1. Casting a creature or Spacecraft creates a 1/1 Officer token. Tap seven Officers: search for a Spacecraft and put it onto the battlefield.
"The white mythic that takes over the game. Every spell you cast grows your board, and the anthem makes your tokens into real threats." — GameTyrant
U.S.S. Enterprise-A, Free Spirit
3WWMythic
Ward 2. ETB: create three 1/1 Officer tokens. Station: tap creatures to power it up. At 8+ charge counters it becomes a 5/5 flying creature.
Three tokens on ETB is already strong. Then it becomes a 5/5 flyer. One of the best top-end threats in sealed.
Deanna Troi, Psychic Counsel
1WRare
Other creatures you control enter with an additional +1/+1 counter on them.
"A 2-mana creature that permanently buffs everything you play afterwards. Hardcastle-level snowball in sealed." — MTGRocks
Excalbian Gamemaster
5WMythic
Strange New Worlds: ETB create X Officer tokens (X = differently named lands). Whenever a creature you control enters, untap target permanent.
In sealed with diverse mana bases, this regularly creates 4-5 tokens. Each token untaps a permanent. Devastating with Spacecraft.
The Needs of the Many
1WRare
Sacrifice a creature: all your creatures gain hexproof + indestructible. You gain hexproof and prevent all damage. Exiles itself.
"The best combat trick in the set and it's not close. Sacrifice your worst creature, save your entire board from a wrath or alpha strike." — GameTyrant

Blue Bombs

Blue has the deepest bomb pool in TRK. Multiple mythics that warp the game, plus Wesley Crusher as one of the best rares in the entire set.

Mr. Spock, Science Officer
2UUMythic
If you would draw a card, draw two cards instead.
"The simplest text, the biggest impact. Spock turns every draw step, every cantrip, every dilemma into double the cards. You will bury your opponent in resources." — GameTyrant
Wesley Crusher, Starfleet Cadet
2UURareFlash
Flash. Choose TWO: counter noncreature unless pay 2 / bounce MV≤2 / unblockable creature / draw 2 discard 2.
"A flash 4-drop that does two things is absurd. This is a first pick over most rares." — MTGRocks
Kobayashi Maru Scenario
1UUMythic
Each upkeep, choose a new mode: bounce creature MV≤3 / draw two cards / make creatures unblockable / counter next spell. Can't repeat choices.
Every mode is excellent. By the time you've used all four, you've so far outpaced your opponent that the game is over. Classic sealed bomb: does everything.
Scotty, Miracle Worker
1UUMythic
During your main phase, each card in your graveyard has beam me up (cast from graveyard by returning a creature to hand).
"Turns your graveyard into a second hand. In a long sealed game, Scotty generates absurd card advantage. Every removal spell, every trick — you get to reuse them all." — MTGRocks
Subcommander T'Pol
2URareFlash
Flash. Choose: counter activated/triggered ability / tap creature + stun counter.
"A Stifle or a tap-down on a body at flash speed. This card creates impossible combat situations." — MTGRocks

Black Bombs

Black has the best individual finishers. Khan and Georgiou are both mythics that end games fast, and the Borg enchantment is a one-card combo.

Khan, Vengeful Heart
2BBMythic
5/3. ETB: sacrifice another creature or artifact to draw two cards. You may cast Khan from exile.
"A 5/3 that draws two cards on ETB. And if they exile it, you cast it again. Khan is nearly impossible to answer permanently — the definition of a sealed bomb." — GameTyrant
Georgiou, Terran Emperor
4BBMythic
4/6. You don't lose at 0 life. At end step, lose 3 life and draw a card. At 0 or less life, create ten 1/1 Officer tokens.
A 4/6 that draws you a card every turn. When you hit 0 life, instead of dying you get ten tokens. The mirror universe version of a sealed bomb.
Death Is Irrelevant
6BBMythic
Artifact creatures you control have indestructible. At end step, assimilate a creature card from any graveyard (put it onto the battlefield as a Borg).
"If this resolves, the game is over in 2-3 turns. Your Borg can't die, and every creature that dies on either side joins your army." — MTGRocks
Gul Dukat, Colonial Prefect
4BBBMythic
6/6 menace. At each end step, each opponent loses life equal to the life they lost this turn. You gain that much life.
Doubles all life loss your opponent takes. Any damage they receive during combat or from spells hits them again at end step. A 6/6 menace that also drains is a fast clock.
Armus, Skin of Evil
5BBRare
6/6 menace. At end step, target opponent discards two cards. Discard from hand: -4/-4 to a creature.
A 6/6 menace that forces discard every turn. Even from your hand it doubles as removal. In sealed's longer games, the discard lock is devastating.

Red Bombs

Red's bombs reward aggression and chaos. Kirk is the headliner, and the supporting cast of Q and Worf provide haymaker-level plays.

Captain James T. Kirk
2RMythic
Whenever Kirk enters or attacks, choose one (if empty-handed, choose one or more): loot / create 1/1 Officer / creatures can't block this turn.
"Kirk does everything and he does it every time he attacks. The 'can't block' mode wins games out of nowhere, and hellbent mode lets you choose all three." — GameTyrant
Q, Stacking the Deck
2RRMythic
4/4. ETB: shuffle, exile cards until you hit a nonland. That card gains "exile a card from hand: create a copy. You may cast copies without paying mana costs."
"Q is a slot machine that always pays out. You exile cards from hand to copy whatever you hit — and you cast those copies for free. In sealed's slower games, Q generates absurd value." — MTGRocks
Worf, Intelligence Contractor
2RRare
ETB: for each opponent, gain control of a creature they control with MV 3 or less until EOT. Untap them. They become Warriors with haste.
Threaten effects are already good in sealed. Worf steals the best small creature, turns it into a Warrior, and swings immediately. Devastating tempo play at 3 mana.
Will Riker, First in the Field
3RRare
4/3. At beginning of combat, reveal cards until you hit two lands. Put those lands onto the battlefield, rest on bottom.
"Free ramp every combat. In a format built around Strange New Worlds landfall triggers, Riker is a guaranteed value engine. Fuels Gruul landfall perfectly." — GameTyrant
Brazen Bluff
RRRare
Target opponent's noncreature spell: they exile it or you free-cast a noncreature from hand.
"Pure mindgames. Either you counter their spell or you get to cheat something into play. Both outcomes are backbreaking." — GameTyrant

Green Bombs

Green's bombs are value engines that snowball through exploration, counters, and ramp. Janeway is the format-defining mythic.

Captain Kathryn Janeway
3GMythic
Play an additional land each turn. Whenever Janeway or another creature enters, that creature explores.
"The card that makes 5-color soup a real strategy in sealed. Extra lands plus free explores on every creature means you're always ahead on cards and mana." — GameTyrant
Dr. Joseph M'Benga
2GGMythic
Other creatures with counters have hexproof. Doubles all counters placed on your creatures.
"In a set full of +1/+1 counters, explore, and assimilate, M'Benga turns every counter into two and protects everything that has one. A sealed build-around that works with your whole pool." — MTGRocks
Dr. Leonard "Bones" McCoy
1GGRareFlash
Flash. Choose: return non-Doctor permanent from GY to hand / target creature returns with +1/+1 counter when it dies this turn.
"Recursion or death insurance on a flash body. Card advantage machine that's never bad." — GameTyrant
Captain Pike, Fullness of Time
1GGRare
Power/toughness equal to your differently named lands. Look at top card any time. Play lands from top of library.
In sealed with diverse mana bases, Pike is regularly a 4/4 or 5/5 for 3 mana. Playing lands off the top means you never miss land drops. Pairs perfectly with Strange New Worlds.
U.S.S. Discovery, at Black Alert
3GGRare
Whenever Discovery enters or attacks, discover 5 (free-cast a nonland card with MV 5 or less from the top of your library).
"A 5/5 Spacecraft that casts a free spell every time it attacks. In sealed, the discover 5 range hits almost every bomb in your deck." — MTGRocks

Multicolor Bombs

Gold cards are harder to cast but the payoffs are enormous. Strange New Worlds lands make splashing very realistic in this format.

Borg Cube
5WBMythic
Affinity for artifact creatures. Whenever it enters or attacks, assimilate a creature exiled with it. If you can't, exile a creature instead. 6/6 Spacecraft.
"The Borg Cube exiles a creature, then brings it back under your control as a Borg. If you have any artifact creatures, it comes down ahead of curve. The best gold bomb in the set." — GameTyrant
Kira Nerys, Firebrand
1RWRare
4/3 trample. Creatures you control enter tapped and attacking if you control an attacker. Each entering creature gets +1/+1 until EOT.
Kira turns every creature you play mid-combat into a hasty attacker. In sealed with token generators, she creates devastating alpha strikes.
Gowron, Eager for Battle
2BRRare
3/3. Whenever another creature dies, +1/+1 counter. Sacrifice a creature: trample (if Klingon, draw a card too).
"Gowron grows from every trade, every removal spell, every combat. In a sealed format with tokens everywhere, he gets huge fast." — MTGRocks
Live Long and Prosper
5WURare
Can't be countered. Double your life total. Draw cards equal to your hand size. No max hand size until next turn.
Seven mana is a lot, but this card wins the game. Double your life and refill your hand. In sealed's grindy games, this is the ultimate comeback card.

Colorless & Artifacts

Colorless bombs go in any deck. These are the cards that make you not care what colors you opened.

Crystalline Entity
8Mythic
7/7 flying. When it enters (if cast), destroy all nonartifact creatures.
"A one-sided wrath on a 7/7 flyer. Yes, it costs 8. In sealed, you will get to 8 mana. And when you do, the game is over." — GameTyrant
Deep Space 9
9Mythic
ETB and each upkeep: exile top 4, put all permanents onto the battlefield. Station: becomes a Spacecraft creature at 8+ counters.
The ultimate top-end. Four free permanents every upkeep. Unbeatable if it survives a turn cycle. Sealed decks can ramp to 9 with Strange New Worlds and green.
U.S.S. Enterprise-D, Galaxy-Class
3Rare
Whenever charge counters are added (first time each turn), exile top card and you may play it. Station: becomes 4/5 creature at 8+ counters.
"The most splashable Spacecraft in the set. Goes in any deck, generates free cards, and eventually becomes a 4/5 flyer. Premium rare." — MTGRocks

Multicolor & Colorless — Sealed Payoffs

Multicolor cards are harder to cast but often more powerful. In sealed with Strange New Worlds fixing, they're very playable.

"The Strange New Worlds lands make 3-color decks genuinely consistent. Don't be afraid to splash gold cards — the fixing is there." — MTGRocks, TRK Sealed Guide
Test of Priorities
1UUncommon
Choose: surveil 2 + draw / exile your creature, return at end step
The flicker mode reuses powerful ETBs (Bones, T'Pol, Saavik) and dodges removal. The draw mode is never dead.
Test of Identity
1RUncommon
Choose: target Klingon gets +1/+1 and double strike / discard 1, draw 2
Double strike on a Klingon is lethal. The loot mode ensures it's never a dead card even without Klingons.
Test of Courage
GUncommon
Choose: exile target card from graveyard + gain 2 life / destroy artifact or enchantment MV≤3
Maindeckable artifact/enchantment removal at 1 mana. The graveyard hate mode shuts down recursion strategies.
